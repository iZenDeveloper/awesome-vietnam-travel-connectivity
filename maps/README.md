## 🗺 How to Import to Google My Maps in 60 Seconds:
1. Go to [Google My Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/d/) and click **"+ Create a New Map"**.
2. Under the untitled layer, click **"Import"**.
3. Drag and drop `vietnam_5g_tourist_safety_map_2026.kml` (or `.geojson`).
4. Set the map title to: `Vietnam 5G Viettel & Tourist Safety Map 2026 - By Kovitel`.
5. Click **"Share"** > Turn on **"Anyone with this link can view"**.
6. Copy your public link! (Permanent DR 98 link on Google Maps).
