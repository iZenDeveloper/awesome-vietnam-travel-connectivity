### How to Publish to Issuu:
1. Open `vietnam_travel_connectivity_magazine_2026.html` in Chrome/Safari.
2. Print to PDF (`Cmd+P` or `Ctrl+P`) > Save as `Vietnam_Travel_Connectivity_Magazine_2026.pdf`.
3. Upload to [Issuu.com](https://issuu.com).
4. Permanent DR 93 flipbook publication with clickable affiliate and brand links to `https://kovitel.com`.
