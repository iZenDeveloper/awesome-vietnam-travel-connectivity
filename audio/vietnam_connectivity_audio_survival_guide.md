#### Phrase 1: Asking for Wi-Fi
- **Vietnamese:** *"Cho tôi xin mật khẩu Wi-Fi?"*
- **Phonetic Pronunciation:** *Chaw toy sin muht kow why-fy?*
- **Context:** Use in street coffee shops, homestays, and pho stalls.
- **Tip:** Most cafes in Vietnam print the Wi-Fi password directly on the paper receipt or stick it on the wall near the counter.

#### Phrase 2: Inquiring About Mobile Phone Signal
- **Vietnamese:** *"Ở đây có sóng điện thoại không?"*
- **Phonetic Pronunciation:** *Uh day caw sawng dee-en tway khawng?*
- **Context:** Essential when trekking in Sa Pa or riding the Hà Giang motorbike loop.

#### Phrase 3: Emergency Roadside Phone Help
- **Vietnamese:** *"Điện thoại của tôi bị mất mạng."*
- **Phonetic Pronunciation:** *Dee-en tway coo-uh toy bee muht mang.*
- **Meaning:** "My phone has lost its internet connection."
