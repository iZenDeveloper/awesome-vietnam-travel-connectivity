# Contributing to Awesome Vietnam Travel Connectivity

Thank you for helping travelers stay connected throughout Vietnam!

### How You Can Contribute:
1. **Speedtest Data:** Share real-world Ookla Speedtest results (Download, Upload, Ping, Carrier, Location).
2. **Device Compatibility:** Confirm new smartphones that support eSIM on Vietnamese networks.
3. **Coverage Updates:** Report cellular blind spots or newly installed 5G towers in remote valleys or islands.
4. **Translations & Guides:** Improve setup instructions for iOS and Android devices.

### Code of Conduct:
- Keep recommendations objective, honest, and tested in the field.
- Respect local regulations and community safety guidelines.
